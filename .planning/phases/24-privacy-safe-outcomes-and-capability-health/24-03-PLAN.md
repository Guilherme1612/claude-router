<objective>
Wave 3: turn outcome history + the Phase 22 relationship graph + the Phase 21 registry into the HLTH-08/09/10 health observation catalog, and ship the full HLTH-05 admin surface (inspect/reset/dispose/recover).

Plan 24-02 produced per-capability outcome records + usefulness scores. This plan adds the catalog that derives 10 observation kinds (missing_category, missing_dependency, unmapped, stale, long_unused, duplicate, overlap, complementary, ineffective, reusable_workflow) from three inputs: the registry (Phase 21), the relationship graph (Phase 22), and outcome history (Plan 24-02). It also completes the admin CLI: Plan 24-01 shipped `router health inspect`; this plan adds reset/dispose/recover, all operating exclusively on ~/.claude/router/health/ and verified by a content-hash isolation regression test.

Scope note (W5 deferral): `reusable_workflow` in this plan is per-capability — a chain of consecutive 'completed' outcomes for ONE capability_id. Cross-capability multi-step workflow pattern mining (A→B→C→D→E completion sequences promotable to a new skill/agent) is explicitly deferred per the 24-CONTEXT.md `<deferred>` section: Phase 24 ships the per-capability conservative baseline; cross-capability route_id-correlation mining needs more evidence than Phase 24 will have collected and lands in a future phase.

Purpose: The catalog is what Phase 25's `/router suggestion` reads to surface one high-value recommendation. The admin surface is what lets a user inspect, reset, dispose of, and recover health state without ever touching the authoritative registry or the active routing map (HLTH-05).

Output: src/health/catalog.mjs, src/health/admin.mjs (extended), src/cli/router-control.mjs (extended), two test files. HLTH-05/08/09/10 green.

Load-bearing decisions (Claude's discretion, grounded in 24-RESEARCH.md + 24-PATTERNS.md):
- D-2 (HLTH-08 edge mapping): The Phase 22 RELATIONSHIP_TYPES (src/registry/relationships.mjs line 7-16) are substitute|variant|prerequisite|composition|conflict|fallback|implementation|alias — none is named duplicate/overlap/complementary. The mapping is: substitute→duplicate, variant→overlap, composition→complementary. The catalog reads already-derived relationship edges (deriveRelationships output), it does NOT re-derive relationships. The other 6 HLTH-08 kinds come from the registry + contracts (missing_category, missing_dependency, unmapped, stale) and outcome history (long_unused, ineffective). reusable_workflow comes from outcome-history sequence detection (HLTH-09). This mapping is confirmed by reading relationships.mjs end-to-end (line 7-16 RULES keys, RELATIONSHIP_TYPES = Object.keys(RULES).sort()).
- D-4 (CLI surface, reaffirmed): `router health inspect|reset|dispose|recover` — exactly four subcommands (UX-09 forbids a "large family of maintenance commands"; four is the bounded set HLTH-05 names).
- D-5 (scope isolation, enforced): admin.mjs must NOT import src/registry/activate.mjs or src/prompt/publish-index.mjs. healthRoot = join(ownedRoot, 'health') — sibling of evidence/. The content-hash regression test in this plan is the enforcement gate.
- D-6 (name collision, reaffirmed): observation records use the field `outcome_kind` where they reference outcome history; the catalog's own kind field is `observation_kind` (parallel naming, never bare `outcome`).
</objective>
