<objective>
Wire lease authority into the router hot path with revocation precedence (LEASE-04) and promote the in-memory idempotency Set to a durable on-lease checkpoint claim for at-most-once resume (LEASE-05). This plan consumes the store + policy modules from Plan 01 and threads them into the existing evaluateAuthorityHint hot path and the claude.mjs resume path without changing the evaluateAuthorityPolicy sealed signature.

Purpose: LEASE-04 makes revocation authoritative (a revoked lease blocks further work even with high confidence and cached recommendations); LEASE-05 makes resume survive compaction/restart (the Phase 38 in-memory Set is lost on re-spawn). Both are elevation-of-privilege threats if absent.

Output: resolveLeaseAuthority in policy.mjs, claimCheckpoint/releaseCheckpoint in store.mjs, evaluateAuthorityHint lease consultation in router.mjs, durable resume in claude.mjs, and two adversarial test files proving revocation precedence and at-most-once resume across a simulated restart.
</objective>
