<objective>
Wave 4: version and canary-guard every health threshold, decay constant, cooldown, and calibration corpus version (HLTH-11).

Plans 24-01/02/03 shipped the schema, observer, scorer, catalog, and admin with inline constants (HALF_LIFE_MS, MINIMUM_SAMPLES, the score weights, the tier thresholds). This plan extracts those constants into a versioned thresholds module (policy_version strings), wires threshold activation through the existing canary-controller.mjs gate suite (so a stale or under-evidenced threshold is never promoted), and adds multilingual calibration plumbing (the broader corpus is deferred per CONTEXT.md).

Purpose: HLTH-11 requires that every threshold, sample floor, decay constant, cooldown, and calibration be versioned, testable, locally derived, and canary-guarded. Phase 26 (REL-03) makes the health policy a tuple member; the canary gate is what makes that publication safe. Phase 24 ships the gate adapter so Phase 26 only has to wire the tuple member, not build the guard.

Output: src/health/thresholds.mjs, src/health/canary-bridge.mjs, src/health/score.mjs (constants refactored to import from thresholds.mjs), tests/router.health.canary.test.mjs. HLTH-11 green.

- D-canary (HLTH-11): delegate ALL threshold activation through the existing src/evolution/canary-controller.mjs evaluateCandidate + applyCanaryDecision + REQUIRED_GATES (6 gates: safety, privacy, quality, context_budget, compatibility, latency).
