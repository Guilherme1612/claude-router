<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| Caller data to capsule schema | Untrusted facts and references enter persisted state. |
| Owned root to filesystem | Paths, symlinks, permissions, and partial writes can redirect or corrupt persistence. |
| Corrupt bytes to diagnostics | Attacker-controlled persisted bytes must not be reflected. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-15-01 | Information Disclosure | capsule canonicalization | high | mitigate | Strict allowlist, deterministic limits, and canary assertions across active/LKG/errors. |
| T-15-02 | Tampering | capsule persistence | high | mitigate | Containment, lstat symlink rejection, restrictive mode, durable temp write, fsync, atomic rename, validated LKG. |
| T-15-03 | Denial of Service | validation and parsing | medium | mitigate | Pre-read byte ceilings and field/count limits before canonicalization. |
| T-15-04 | Spoofing | workflow identity | medium | mitigate | Stable composite identity derived from canonical scope/kind/goal/position rather than labels. |
</threat_model>
