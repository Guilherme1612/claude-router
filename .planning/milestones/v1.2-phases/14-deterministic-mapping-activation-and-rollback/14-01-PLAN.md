## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| Phase 13 candidate to mapper | Candidate and reconciliation evidence may be malformed, stale, reordered, or inconsistent. |
| Advisory resolver to deterministic policy | Background evidence is untrusted and must never gain activation or safety authority. |
| Mapping report to activation/CLI | Downstream consumers rely on canonical evidence, target IDs, and fingerprints. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-14-01 | Tampering | `mapCandidateRegistry` candidate join | high | mitigate | Validate/canonicalize the supplied candidate, require eligible reconciliation fingerprint agreement, build lookup only from those exact bytes, and reapply every invocability predicate before emitting a target. |
| T-14-02 | Elevation of Privilege | advisory evidence and lexical tier | high | mitigate | Encode non-overriding authority tiers; advisory evidence cannot outrank explicit/identity evidence, change permissions/scope/dependencies, or mutate active mappings. |
| T-14-03 | Spoofing | route-family continuity claims | high | mitigate | Accept inheritance only from authoritative continuity or existing mapping tied to the same stable identity; reject name/token/fingerprint-only continuity. |
| T-14-04 | Repudiation | mapping explanation | medium | mitigate | Include policy/candidate fingerprints, ordered accepted/rejected evidence, winning rule, alternatives, score, margin, and stable reason codes in every result. |
| T-14-05 | Information Disclosure | evidence ledger/calibration output | medium | mitigate | Recursively strip local/absolute paths and prohibit raw prompts/secrets while preserving portable logical provenance. |
| T-14-06 | Denial of Service | unbounded evidence/token input | medium | mitigate | Apply bounded arrays/token lengths, deterministic normalization, duplicate collapse, and input-size validation before scoring. |
</threat_model>
